# M03-B — About & Contact

## Scope
- Four static, bilingual pages: `/about/`, `/es/sobre-mi/`, `/contact/`, `/es/contacto/`.
- Two reusable, locale-aware components and one shared CSS module.
- Localized titles, descriptions, canonical and hreflang metadata.
- Footer links now lead to the real About and Contact routes.
- No contact form or API: mailto links for `booking@notanangeldj.com` and `contact@notanangeldj.com`.

## Editorial review required
The About copy is a draft based on the project's background and must be reviewed for personal accuracy, tone and professional claims. The emails are supplied by the project owner. No response times, rates, clients or performances are claimed.
