A loudspeaker system converts an electrical audio signal into sound. Understanding the difference between a speaker, an amplifier and a monitor helps prevent incorrect connections.

## Active and passive systems

An active speaker includes amplification and normally accepts a suitable line-level input. A passive speaker requires an external power amplifier. A mixer output must not be connected directly to an unpowered passive loudspeaker expecting amplified power.

## Booth and audience

Booth monitors help the DJ hear the mix at the workstation. A PA system distributes sound to the audience. Monitor placement, safe volume and hearing protection matter as much as the connector type.

## Practice

Identify whether your speakers are active or passive, which inputs they accept, how they are powered and where the booth/master signals originate.
