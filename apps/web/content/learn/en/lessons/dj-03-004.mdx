Gain, EQ, filter and fader controls change different aspects of the signal. Their jobs are related, but they are not substitutes for one another.

## Gain staging

Input gain sets the level before later processing; excessive gain can overload a stage even when the master output is turned down. Maintain headroom, monitor meters and avoid sustained red or clipping indications.

## Tone and level controls

EQ alters frequency bands, filters shape the frequency spectrum, channel faders control the channel contribution and a crossfader can blend assigned channels. The order and behaviour vary by mixer.

## Practice

With safe monitoring levels, adjust input gain first, then return EQ and filters to neutral. Observe the channel meter while changing the channel fader and master. Identify which controls change level at each stage.
