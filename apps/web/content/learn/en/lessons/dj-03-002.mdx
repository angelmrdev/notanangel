A mixer is the routing centre of many DJ systems: audio enters through sources, is assigned to channels, can be monitored privately and leaves through one or more outputs. This lesson will follow the signal rather than start with knob techniques.

## Signal route

A channel input may accept line or phono sources, depending on the mixer. The incoming signal passes through input selection and level controls, may pass through EQ and fader stages, and is then routed to the main mix. Exact ordering varies by model; consult the mixer block diagram.

## Independent monitoring

Headphone cue is commonly a separate monitoring bus. The booth output is typically a monitor feed, not another playback deck. Main/master audio goes to the audience system.
