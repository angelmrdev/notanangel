**Music Theory for DJs** builds the musical listening skills that make mixing intentional. You will learn to recognize where a track is going, not just what its BPM display says.

## What you will learn

- Pulse, tempo and BPM.
- Beats, bars, time signatures and phrase counting.
- Intros, verses, choruses, breakdowns, builds and drops.
- Downbeats, accents and transients.
- Keys, scales and the Camelot Wheel.
- Energy, dynamics, groove and swing.

## How to study this module

Follow the nine lessons in order. Use music you know, tap the beat, count phrases aloud and revisit sections that challenge you. Software analysis is a tool, not a substitute for listening.

<PracticeExercise title="Listening habit">Choose one track a day and identify its pulse, downbeats, phrase boundaries, principal sections and energy changes. Write your observations before checking the software display.</PracticeExercise>

## After this module

Apply these listening skills to equipment, software and hands-on mixing lessons later in the course.
