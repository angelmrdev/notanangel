Build your DJ collection as a **reliable, searchable and portable system**, rather than a pile of folders. This module connects legal music access and file quality with practical organization and track preparation.

## Learning outcomes

- Compare legitimate sources and understand licence limitations.
- Check audio formats and metadata before importing.
- Organize files and playlists without losing track references.
- Inspect beatgrids, prepare cue points and develop practical tags.
- Protect files and preparation data with tested backups.

## Suggested workflow

Complete the nine lessons in order. Start with a small set of tracks you can lawfully use and keep a backup before changing their location or tags. The advanced planning of a full DJ set belongs to a later module.

## Before you begin

Record where your audio files live, where the DJ software stores its database, and which streaming services—if any—are integrated. Do not assume that a streaming cache is a portable audio file.
