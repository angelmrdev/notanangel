Headphone cueing lets a DJ monitor a channel without necessarily sending it to the audience. That private path is fundamental to preparing music safely.

## Cue and master signals

Cue selects the channels to monitor. Cue/master blend can balance private prelisten with the main mix, depending on the mixer. Headphone volume affects listening comfort and is not a replacement for channel gain.

## Important distinction

A track can be playing in the software yet remain inaudible through the master if its channel is closed. Conversely, cue monitoring does not necessarily require opening the main fader.

## Practice

At a low listening level, identify a channel cue selector, headphone volume and any cue/master control. Describe where each signal goes.
