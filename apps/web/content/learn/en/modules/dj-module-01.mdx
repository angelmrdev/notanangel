Welcome to **DJ Basics**, the starting point for learning to DJ. This module introduces the craft before asking you to master beatmatching or more advanced performance techniques.

## What you will learn

- What DJs actually do, from music selection to transitions.
- How DJ culture developed and why different performance traditions matter.
- How club, open-format, radio, mobile and turntablist approaches differ.
- How a deck, mixer, audio interface, sound system and headphone cue fit together.
- The vocabulary needed to follow later lessons.
- How to connect and test a basic DJ setup safely.

## How to study this module

Work through the six lessons in order, then repeat the practical exercises using either actual hardware or diagrams and listening exercises. You do not need to purchase professional club equipment to begin.

<KeyConcept title="Your first milestone">You should be able to explain a basic two-deck setup, recognize its signal paths and describe why you would play one track after another.</KeyConcept>

## After this module

Move on to **Music Theory for DJs** to develop the listening skills that make transitions sound natural: tempo, bars, phrases, song structure, musical keys and groove.
