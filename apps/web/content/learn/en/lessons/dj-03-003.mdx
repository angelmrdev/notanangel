Deck controls translate physical gestures into playback operations. A jog wheel may be used to search, bend timing or scratch when the hardware and mode support it; the effect is not identical on every system.

## Essential transport controls

PLAY/PAUSE starts or stops playback. CUE may return to a stored starting point or allow temporary preview, depending on configuration. Jog mode determines how wheel movements affect a track.

## Pitch and tempo

The tempo fader changes playback rate. On some systems, key lock or master tempo can reduce pitch changes when the rate changes, but it can introduce artifacts. A display value is not a substitute for listening.

## Practice

Find and label PLAY, CUE, jog wheel, tempo fader and tempo-range selector on your device. Describe each function without changing the audience output.
