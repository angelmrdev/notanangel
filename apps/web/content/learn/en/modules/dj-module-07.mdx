This module builds on **Module 06: Beatmatching Fundamentals**. Keeping two songs in time is only the starting point: you also need to manage their frequency balance, loudness and musical energy.

## Learning outcomes

- Distinguish EQ from filters, and understand what each control changes.
- Keep transitions at controlled levels with enough headroom.
- Hand off bass without layering competing kicks and basslines.
- Choose between a smooth blend, a short transition and a clean cut.
- Evaluate three complete transitions using listening, not visuals alone.

## Recommended practice

Use two or three legally obtained tracks with clear phrases and stable tempo. Work at a safe listening level. Practise first with the same two songs you used in Module 06, then test combinations that require a different transition.

## Course boundaries

Module 06 covers tempo and beat alignment; this module focuses on **tonal balance, levels, and musical transitions**. Detailed creative effects and performance routines are reserved for subsequent modules.

## Lesson roadmap

1. **EQ Basics for DJs** — Understand low, mid and high frequency bands, and recognise when EQ changes improve or damage a mix.

2. **Gain, Levels and Headroom** — Use trim, channel faders and master levels without clipping or sudden volume jumps.

3. **How to Swap Bass in a Transition** — Avoid clashing kick drums and basslines with a deliberate low-frequency handoff.

4. **Blend with EQ and Phrase Timing** — Coordinate channel faders, EQ and musical phrases for a smooth, intentional blend.

5. **Filter Transitions Without Overdoing It** — Understand high-pass and low-pass filters and when a restrained sweep supports a transition.

6. **Cuts, Drops and Quick Transitions** — Choose a clean cut or a short overlap when a long blend is musically unsuitable.

7. **Practice Lab: Three Transitions** — Perform, record and evaluate an EQ blend, a bass swap and a clean cut.
