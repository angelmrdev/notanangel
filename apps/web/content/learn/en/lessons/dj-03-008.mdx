Reliable troubleshooting starts by following the signal path, not by changing every setting at once. Verify power and routing before changing advanced options.

## Safe connection order

With levels down and devices powered appropriately, connect sources to mixer, mixer outputs to the correct inputs and headphones to the designated headphone jack. Confirm the power-on and power-off order specified by your equipment manufacturer; many systems use amplifiers or active speakers last on and first off to reduce unwanted transients.

## Diagnostic method

If there is no sound, check source playback, channel input selection, channel metering, faders, master metering, physical output and speaker power. For hum, inspect cable type, routing and grounding without defeating protective earth. For distortion, check stages from source to output rather than lowering only the final speaker volume.

## Practice

Create a fault worksheet with symptoms, signal stage, test and observed result. Use low levels and stop if there is unsafe heat, smell, smoke or unexpected electrical behaviour.
