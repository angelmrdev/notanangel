**DJ Equipment** takes you beyond recognizing equipment names. You will learn how sound travels through a DJ system, what each hardware control changes, and how to connect and check a setup safely.

## Learning outcomes

- Compare controllers, standalone players, CDJ-style media players and turntables by their workflows, not price.
- Trace input, channel, cue, booth and master routing through a mixer.
- Understand transport controls, jog wheels, pitch faders, EQ, gain and filters.
- Set up headphone monitoring and select suitable speaker connections.
- Identify common cables, balanced connections and audio interfaces.
- Diagnose silence, clipping, hum and incorrect output routing methodically.

## Recommended learning sequence

Work through all eight lessons in order. The opening comparison provides context; later lessons explain each section of the system. Keep your own controller or mixer manual nearby because exact buttons, input levels and connector layouts vary by model.

<PracticeExercise title="Before you start">Write down the equipment you have access to, its output connector types and whether your speakers are active or passive. You will use this inventory in the final troubleshooting lesson.</PracticeExercise>

## Equipment and safety notes

Turn down outputs before wiring or changing connections. Never connect speaker-level power-amplifier outputs to line-level inputs. Do not use adapters to bypass protective earth or attempt mains electrical repair. If in doubt about electrical installation or high-power PA systems, use a qualified technician.

## After this module

You will be ready to explore DJ software, prepare a reliable music library and practise beatmatching with a clear understanding of the physical signal path.
