This module moves from **understanding the controls** to **listening and adjusting two tracks in real time**. Beatmatching is not just matching BPM: you must align the pulse, notice drift and choose when to launch the incoming track.

## Learning outcomes

- Distinguish tempo changes from pitch changes and explain what key lock does.
- Match steady rhythms by ear, using small corrections instead of waveform guesses.
- Understand when BPM Sync and Beat Sync help, and when they can mislead you.
- Prepare a second track in headphones and keep two songs aligned.
- Perform a simple two-song transition with appropriate timing and levels.

## Recommended practice

Use two lawful local files with a steady 4/4 pulse and similar BPM. Start with the master output at a safe level and an isolated headphone cue. Practise without Sync first, then compare the result using Sync. Do not skip listening checks merely because the grid looks aligned.

## Course boundaries
