DJ audio connections are defined by their function and signal level, not only by connector shape. A familiar connector can carry different kinds of signals on different devices.

## Connectors and levels

RCA often appears on consumer line-level and phono connections. XLR and TRS frequently carry balanced line signals, while TS is commonly unbalanced; verify actual wiring and output specifications. USB can carry audio data and control messages rather than analog sound.

## Balanced and unbalanced

Balanced inputs and outputs can help reject common-mode interference when wired correctly. A balanced-looking connector alone does not guarantee a balanced signal. Choose cables by endpoint requirements, not appearance.

## Practice

Draw a connection table identifying input connector, output connector, signal type, level and any required interface or phono preamp.

## Scope

Headroom and avoiding clipping remain essential; product-specific adapters must be validated against manuals.
